Who Are We

Three decades of IT experience, one focus.

01

About Us

Our founders have over three decades of experience in the IT industry and started DataMids to help businesses, large and small, identify, consolidate and understand their existing data for better use in their business decisions.

02

Our Vision

To help businesses make sense of their data and unify it across various channels, in a format that helps decision makers visualize their strengths and weaknesses and make the necessary calibrations.

03

Our Mission

To provide enterprise data management that delivers quality data, in a timely manner, from any channel, and present it in a format that enables our clients to make timely business decisions.

04

Technology

To improve data quality, you must be able to measure your data. Determining what and how to measure is often challenging — we provide a comprehensive, adaptable approach to defining measurable characteristics for quality.
